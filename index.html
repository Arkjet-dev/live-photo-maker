How to get it onto your iPhone

  1. From a Mac: download both files (unzip if you used the zip), select the .JPG and .MOV together and drag them into the Photos app, or AirDrop both at once to your iPhone. They'll show up as one Live Photo.
  2. Made it on your iPhone: tap Photo and Video, save both to Files, then select both in Files, tap Share and AirDrop them to yourself (or to a Mac). Saving them separately to Photos won't pair them.
  3. Keep the two files' names matching and don't edit either one, or they'll stop pairing.
  4. To check: open it in Photos and press and hold the photo; it should play. It says LIVE in the top corner.

First run downloads the converter (about 30 MB) and conversion can take a minute on phones.
